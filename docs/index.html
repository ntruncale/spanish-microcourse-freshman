Español 9: Gramática

A self-paced Spanish grammar course for freshmen who studied Spanish in middle school. One module a week, 30 modules in all. Open the module your teacher assigned in Blackbaud, work through every step, then complete the Blackbaud check.

Cómo funciona · How it works

  • Every module has the same 7 steps: Contexto, Aprende, Practica, Crea, Reflexiona, Evalúa, Más.
  • Tap any 🔊 to hear Spanish. Choose a voice from España or Latinoamérica at the top of each module, and tap 🐢 or choose Lenta to slow it down.
  • Where you type Spanish, tap the á é í ó ú ü ñ ¿ ¡ keys. On the iPad keyboard you can also hold a letter down to choose an accent.
  • Activities check themselves. Yellow means you're right except for an accent mark.
  • Finish every activity to get the completion code for the Blackbaud check.
